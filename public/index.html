Trade and Treachery

Three to five countries, one live market. Some start with industry, some with raw materials, some with almost nothing. Trade, bluff and bargain your way up.

  1. Choose a country. You command one ship, three squares long, somewhere on a big hidden map.
  2. Spend action points. You get more every 30 seconds. Sail forwards or backwards, turn, ping your radar, or fire.
  3. Hunt blind. Radar shows ships near you, and warns them. Shots can land anywhere within range. Both start short: spend points to reach further.
  4. Make and break alliances. Allies can see each other's ships until someone breaks the alliance. Whether they shoot is up to them.
  1. Run one country. Everyone starts with different things, and nobody is told who got what.
  2. Make goods and sell them. Turn timber, ore and wool into goods, and sell them to the market in batches of five.
  3. Trade with each other. A safe swap happens all at once. Anything sent on trust is only as good as the promise behind it.
  4. Grow the most to win. It all runs live against one clock, with no bots: only people.

Each room plays the game it was opened for. Pick the game above before you open a room.
