Trade and Treachery

Three to five countries, one live market. Some start with industry, some with raw materials, some with almost nothing. Trade, bluff and bargain your way up.

  1. Choose your laws. Two or three in each of nine areas. Every law makes some people happy and others angry.
  2. Campaign. Up to three actions each round, paid for with gold. You can't borrow.
  3. Watch the votes come in. The map fills with your colour, and the news tells you how people are reacting.
  4. Win enough votes in your own region to reach the national election. There, votes become seats in the council. Win the most seats, or make a deal with another party, to win the game.
  1. Run one country. Everyone starts with different things, and nobody is told who got what.
  2. Make goods and sell them. Turn timber, ore and wool into goods, and sell them to the market in batches of five.
  3. Trade with each other. A safe swap happens all at once. Anything sent on trust is only as good as the promise behind it.
  4. Grow the most to win. It all runs live against one clock, with no bots: only people.
